# Product

## Purpose and audience

Introduce the Boundaries of the Brain laboratory at the University of Malta,
its people, research, publications, and open resources. Help prospective students
and collaborators find relevant work and contact the lab. Explain neuroimaging
and neural cartography clearly for wider readers.

The lab and the original BOB funded project are distinct. Include lab-relevant
outputs with accurate attribution rather than an unfiltered personal bibliography.

## Platform

A dependency-free Node build produces ordinary static HTML/CSS in dist/. No CMS,
database, application server, browser JavaScript, external fonts, or tracking is
required. Content and navigation work without JavaScript. GitHub Pages is the
selected host; the build supports both a project path and a custom-domain root.

## Content

The core pages are Home, People, Research, Publications, and Contact. Research
entries have local detail pages with relevant outputs and public resource links.
Publications distinguish journal articles, preprints, datasets, conference outputs,
and policy responses. People are grouped as Faculty, Lab members, and Alumni.
Faculty includes academic collaborators and does not imply shared lab leadership.
Alumni receive compact text recognition without portraits.

Only approved public records and assets belong in this repository. Maintain
public provenance and licence attribution; prepare internal editorial work in
the separate private workspace. Unknown facts remain unset. Do not advertise
recruitment openings without explicit approval.

## Design commitments

Preserve the supplied BOB logos and the clear teal research directory in DESIGN.md.
Use real research imagery with accurate captions and sources. Keep reading,
navigation, contact, and optional duck discoveries accessible by keyboard and on
mobile screens. The homepage uses the approved cortical banner; paper figures
retain their scientific colours and scales.
